## Wave Gate

All waves are gated by the certified v1.0 baseline unless explicitly approved
for planning-only work.

| Wave 5 | Distribution and commerce expansion | V11-BL-006, V11-BL-007, V11-BL-021 | public integration is stable | provider metadata or integrations validated |
| Wave 6 | Multimedia and accessibility expansion | V11-BL-015, V11-BL-018 | core publication flow is stable | accessibility and multimedia evidence improved |

## Elimination Rule

Waves that are unnecessary after real audit may be removed or deferred. This
must be recorded in the roadmap history.
