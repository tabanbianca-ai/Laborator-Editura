## Purpose

Laborator Editura, with Standard 21 defining the ongoing governance of the
standards catalog, lifecycle, dependencies, versions, compliance, and review
calendar.

## Certification Process

The certification process follows:

1. Inventory.
2. Duplication Analysis.
3. Consolidation.
4. Gap Analysis.
5. Architecture Validation.
6. Security Validation.
7. Quality Validation.
8. Documentation Validation.
9. Compliance Validation.
10. Final Certification.
11. Release v1.0.

## Certification Conditions

Codex v1.0 may be certified only when:

- Mandatory standards are implemented.
- Mandatory modules are documented.
- Mandatory tests have passing or retained evidence where available.
- Critical risks are closed or formally accepted.
- Documentation is complete enough for baseline certification.
- Audits are completed.
- Traceability is established.
- No major architectural violations block certification.
