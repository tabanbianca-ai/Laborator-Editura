# Canonical Internal Audit Standard

## Purpose

Internal audit verifies whether platform governance, compliance, risks,
controls, documentation, exceptions, standards, and remediation actions are
effective, current, traceable, and evidence-based.

## Audit Scope

Internal audit verifies:

- Module compliance.
- Standard conformance.
- Approved exceptions.
- Open risks.
- Automated controls.
- Documentation.
- Traceability.
- Remediation plans.

## Audit Record

Each audit should preserve:

- `auditId`.
- Scope.
- Auditor.
- Date.
- Criteria.
- Evidence reviewed.
- Findings.
- Risk rating.
- Recommendations.
- Corrective actions.
- Owner.
- Due date.
- Closure evidence.
- Audit information.

## Audit Findings

Finding severities are:

- `LOW`.
- `MEDIUM`.
- `HIGH`.
- `CRITICAL`.

## Audit Rules

- Audits must be independent where practical.
- Audit evidence must be preserved.
- Critical findings require tracked remediation.
- Closed findings must preserve closure evidence.
- Audit history must not be rewritten.
- AI may summarize evidence and propose findings, but authorized humans remain
  responsible for audit conclusions.
