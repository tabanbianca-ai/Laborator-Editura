# Business Continuity Standard

## Purpose

Business continuity defines how the platform sustains essential editorial and
operational work during service disruption or degraded infrastructure.

## Continuity Plan Requirements

The continuity plan must define:

- Minimum functions that must remain available.
- Temporary degraded work mode.
- Suspended operations.
- Temporary manual procedures.
- User communication.
- Protection of editorial work.
- Phased recovery.
- Data reconciliation.
- Return to normal operation.

## Continuity Scope

Continuity planning must cover:

- Web application.
- PWA and future mobile clients.
- API services.
- Reader services.
- Publishing services.
- Administration services.
- Backup and restore operations.
- Critical integrations.
- Communication procedures.

## Continuity Rules

- Continuity procedures must not bypass authentication or authorization.
- Manual procedures must be reconciled and audited.
- Publication and distribution may be suspended if rights, metadata,
  preflight, or integrity validation is unavailable.
- AI actions may be restricted under continuity mode.
- Users must receive accurate operational information according to approved
  communication policy.
