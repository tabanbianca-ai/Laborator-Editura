# Canonical Audio and Video Accessibility Standard

## Purpose

This document defines audio and video accessibility requirements under
Standard 12.

## Audio Requirements

Audio materials must support:

- Complete transcript.
- Chapters and markers.
- Playback speed control.
- Resume playback.
- Segment navigation.
- Accessible volume control.
- Linkage between transcript and audio version.
- Speaker identification where relevant.

For speech synthesis content, preserve:

- Source text.
- Source text version.
- Voice profile.
- Provider.
- Model version.
- Approval record.

## Video Requirements

Video materials must support:

- Synchronized captions.
- Captions for relevant sounds.
- Transcript.
- Speaker identification.
- Audio description when visual information is essential.
- Full keyboard control.
- No uncontrolled autoplay.
- Ability to stop problematic animations.
- WebVTT and SRT export.

## Validation Requirements

Validation must check:

- Caption presence.
- Caption synchronization.
- Transcript completeness.
- Audio description need and presence.
- Keyboard-operable controls.
- Reduced-motion compatibility.
- Source version linkage.
- Rights and approval metadata.

## AI Rules

AI may propose transcripts, captions, descriptions, and timing.

AI may not approve accessibility compliance or publish audio/video outputs.
