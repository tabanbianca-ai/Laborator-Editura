# Publication Withdrawal Policy

## Purpose

Withdrawal removes or suspends public availability without deleting publication
history, evidence, files, metadata, rights records, distribution history, or
audit records.

## Withdrawal Reasons

Allowed withdrawal reasons include:

- Rights expiration.
- License revocation.
- Critical error.
- Unauthorized content.
- Legal violation.
- Security issue.
- Superseded version.
- Editorial decision.

## Withdrawal Flow

The canonical withdrawal flow is:

```text
Withdrawal request
  -> Impact evaluation
  -> Withdrawal approval
  -> Block new distribution
  -> Withdraw from channels
  -> Update Library
  -> Notify affected parties
  -> Archive evidence
```

## Withdrawal Versus Archival

- `WITHDRAWN` means the edition is no longer publicly available.
- `ARCHIVED` means the edition is preserved for history and audit.
- `SUPERSEDED` may indicate replacement by a later version.
- Physical deletion is allowed only through a separate justified and audited
  process.

## Withdrawal Rules

- Withdrawal must not delete history.
- Withdrawal must preserve the publication package.
- Withdrawal must preserve distribution records.
- Withdrawal must preserve channel responses.
- Withdrawal must preserve rights and approval evidence.
- Withdrawal must preserve audit records.
- AI must not withdraw publications automatically.
- Authorized human approval is required for controlled withdrawal.
