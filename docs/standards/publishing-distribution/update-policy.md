# Publication Update and Correction Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how published editions are corrected, revised, regenerated,
and synchronized without direct editing of distributed files.

## Update Requirements

Modifying a publication requires:

- New master version.
- Change justification.
- Impact analysis.
- Regeneration of affected formats.
- Applicable revalidation.
- New publication version.
- Channel synchronization.
- Preservation of previous version.

Distributed files must not be edited directly.

## Update Types

Allowed canonical update types are:

- `METADATA_ONLY`.
- `TYPOGRAPHICAL_CORRECTION`.
- `EDITORIAL_CORRECTION`.
- `CONTENT_REVISION`.
- `RIGHTS_UPDATE`.
- `ACCESSIBILITY_UPDATE`.
- `FORMAT_REGENERATION`.
- `CRITICAL_CORRECTION`.

## Critical Correction Rule

A critical correction must be able to trigger:

- Reader notification.
- Distribution suspension.
- Withdrawal of the affected version.
- Regeneration of corrected formats.
- New publication version.
- Preservation of evidence.

## Impact Analysis

Update impact analysis must identify:

- Affected publication versions.
- Affected formats.
- Affected languages.
- Affected territories.
- Affected rights records.
- Affected accessibility evidence.
- Affected distribution channels.
- Affected public catalog records.
- Affected commerce metadata.
- Required notifications.
