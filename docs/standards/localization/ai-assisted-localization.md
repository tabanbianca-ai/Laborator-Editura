# Canonical AI-Assisted Localization Rules

## Purpose

This document defines the allowed and forbidden AI behavior for localization
under Standard 11.

## AI May

AI may:

- Propose translations.
- Detect missing localized text.
- Verify terminology consistency.
- Identify inconsistent localized variants.
- Compare translation variants.
- Detect source-language text remaining in another locale.
- Suggest regional formatting fixes.
- Summarize localization gaps.

## AI May Not

AI may not:

- Definitively validate official terminology.
- Publish translations automatically.
- Override approved terminology.
- Hide missing translations.
- Delete conflicting variants silently.
- Replace human approval where approval is required.
- Change Platform Language, Original Language, Authoring Language, or Target
  Language without explicit authorized action.

## Evidence Requirements

AI-assisted localization output must preserve:

- Input locale.
- Target locale.
- Source key or source asset.
- Suggested value.
- Evidence sources.
- Terminology references.
- Confidence where available.
- Human review status.
- Audit reference.

## Human Final Authority

AI localization remains advisory. Authorized humans retain final authority for
official terminology, published localization resources, publication outputs,
rights-sensitive media, and release approval.
