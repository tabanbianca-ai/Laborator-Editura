# Canonical Language, Territory, Format and Channel Rights Standard

## Purpose

This document defines rights separation by language, territory, format, and
distribution channel under Standard 13.

## Language Rights

Rights must be managed separately for every language.

Example:

```text
Original work: French
Romanian translation right: VALIDATED
Spanish translation right: NOT_GRANTED
Portuguese translation right: UNDER_REVIEW
```

Validation for one language does not authorize translation or publication in
another language.

## Territory Rights

Territories must be explicitly represented as:

- Countries.
- Regions.
- Territorial groups.
- Global only when clearly declared.

Absence of territory must not be interpreted as worldwide authorization.

## Format Rights

Formats must distinguish at least:

- Print.
- PDF.
- EPUB.
- Audiobook.
- Podcast.
- Video.
- Educational material.
- Promotional material.
- Accessible format.

Print publication rights do not automatically authorize audio, video, online,
or derivative publication.

## Channel Rights

Channels must distinguish at least:

- Public website.
- Application.
- Owned store.
- Amazon KDP.
- Print services.
- Audio platforms.
- Video platforms.
- Social networks.
- Institutional distribution.

## Rule of Narrowest Scope

When rights overlap, the most restrictive valid rule applies unless a
documented additional authorization expands the scope.
