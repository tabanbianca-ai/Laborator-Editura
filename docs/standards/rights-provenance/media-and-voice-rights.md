# Canonical Media and Voice Rights Standard

## Purpose

This document defines rights requirements for images, illustrations, audio,
video, voices, and synthetic voice use under Standard 13.

## Image and Illustration Rights

Each image or illustration must preserve:

- Creator.
- Source.
- License.
- Modification permission.
- Commercial use permission.
- Required attribution.
- Territories.
- Channels.
- Validity term.
- AI generation restrictions.
- Authorization evidence.

Images found on the internet must not be treated as automatically reusable.

## Voice Rights

For a human or cloned voice, preserve:

- `voice_owner`.
- `consent_record`.
- `allowed_purposes`.
- `allowed_projects`.
- `allowed_languages`.
- `commercial_use`.
- `model_training_allowed`.
- `voice_cloning_allowed`.
- `valid_from`.
- `valid_until`.
- `revocation_terms`.

Voice cloning cannot be activated without explicit and verifiable consent.

## Audio and Video Rights

Audio and video rights must preserve:

- Source text rights.
- Voice or narrator rights.
- Music and sound rights where applicable.
- Image and footage rights.
- Subtitle and transcript rights where applicable.
- Territory, language, format, and channel restrictions.
- Publication approval.

## AI Rules

AI may identify missing media or voice rights metadata.

AI may not infer consent, approve voice cloning, or declare media reusable.
