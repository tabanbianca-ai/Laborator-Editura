## Purpose

This standard defines mandatory rules for identifying, verifying, managing,
and auditing rights attached to every work and asset in Laborator Editura.

No work or derivative asset may be published without valid rights verification.

## Unified Scope

This standard governs:

- Manuscripts.
- Original works.
- Translations.
- Editions.
- Books and magazines.
- Articles.
- Images and illustrations.
- Audio and video materials.
- Human and synthetic voices.
- Fonts and graphic elements.
- Promotional materials.
- AI-generated or AI-modified content.
- Contracts.
- External imports.
- Publications and distribution channels.

## Principles

All rights operations must follow:

- Rights Before Use.
- Provenance by Default.
- No Publication Without Validation.
- No Automatic Legal Assumption.
- Rights Separated by Language, Territory, Format and Channel.
- Complete Traceability.
- Immutable History.
- Reuse of Validated Information.
- Controlled Rights Inheritance.
- Human Review for Legal Decisions.
- Default Blocking When Uncertain.

## Canonical Rights Areas

The canonical model covers:

- Copyright.
- Translation rights.
- Publishing rights.
- Multimedia rights.
- Licenses.
- Contracts.
- Agreements and consent.
- Authorized territories and languages.
- Content provenance.
- Usage restrictions.
- Rights expiration.
- Derived material traceability.

## Non-Goals

This standard does not implement:

- A legal ERP.
- Digital signature providers.
- Billing, invoicing, or royalties.
- Litigation management.
- Database migrations.
- API changes.
- UI changes.
- Docker or staging changes.

Runtime implementation requires separately approved implementation phases.
