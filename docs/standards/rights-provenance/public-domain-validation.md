# Canonical Public Domain Validation Standard

## Purpose

This document defines public domain validation requirements under Standard 13.

## Required Verification

A work may receive `PUBLIC_DOMAIN` status only after documented verification
of:

- Author.
- Date of death where relevant.
- Jurisdiction.
- Protection term.
- Edition used.
- Translation used.
- Added elements.
- Distinct rights.

## Public Domain Boundary

A public domain original work does not automatically make a modern
translation, foreword, illustration, critical edition, recording, cover, or
adaptation free of rights.

Each added element must be evaluated separately.

## Evidence Requirements

Public domain validation must preserve:

- Source references.
- Jurisdiction.
- Dates and calculation basis.
- Edition details.
- Reviewer.
- Verification status.
- Verification date.
- Restrictions or uncertainties.
- Audit linkage.

## Blocking Rule

Ambiguous public domain status blocks publication until reviewed.

AI may assist with research and risk detection, but AI may not make final
public domain determinations.
