# Canonical AI Content Rights Standard

## Purpose

This document defines rights and provenance requirements for AI-generated or
AI-modified resources under Standard 13.

## Required AI Content Metadata

For every AI-generated or AI-modified resource, preserve:

- Agent.
- Model.
- Provider.
- Model version.
- Prompt or prompt reference.
- Source resources.
- Source licenses.
- Provider policy applicable at execution date.
- Verification result.
- Human approval.
- Usage restrictions.

## AI Rights Limits

AI cannot definitively declare that material is free of rights.

AI may:

- Assist discovery.
- Identify missing evidence.
- Detect conflicting metadata.
- Flag source license restrictions.
- Summarize rights risk.

AI may not:

- Issue final legal validation.
- Grant rights.
- Approve publication.
- Override rights restrictions.
- Hide rights uncertainty.

## Source Policy Preservation

AI outputs must preserve the source policies, provider terms, and date-specific
policy references that applied when the output was generated.

If a provider policy changes later, previously generated resources remain
linked to the policy version applicable at execution time.
