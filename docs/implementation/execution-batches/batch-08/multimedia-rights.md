# Multimedia Rights

Multimedia rights are validated independently.

Required rights may include:

- audio.
- video.
- adaptation.
- voice.
- music.
- image.
- illustration.
- AI processing.

PDF or EPUB rights do not automatically grant audiobook, video, voice, music,
image, illustration, or AI processing rights.

Music and sound effects must be registered as governed digital assets with
creator, source, license, commercial use, adaptation, territory, channel,
validity, and credit metadata.
